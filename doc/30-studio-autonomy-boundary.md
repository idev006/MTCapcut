# Studio Autonomy Boundary

วันที่: 2 พฤศจิกายน 2026  
สถานะ: Mandatory architecture and evaluation rule

## กฎหลัก

Codex/ผู้พัฒนามีหน้าที่สร้าง software architecture, contracts, engines, adapters, tests และ observability เท่านั้น ห้ามใช้วิจารณญาณของ Codex เพื่อเลือก creative direction, story structure, shot, source range, timing, rhythm, text treatment, sound treatment หรือ Timeline Plan สำหรับงานที่นำไปประเมินความสามารถของ MTCapcut

Studio evaluation ที่ถูกต้องต้องมี provenance chain:

`user-authored brief/restrictions → versioned brief interpretation → machine observations in SSOT → independent role recommendations → objections/blockers → Clip Director synthesis → Lead Editor decisions → validated Timeline Plan → render`

ทุก creative decision ต้องมี actor เป็น MTCapcut engine/provider model, engine/prompt/model/config version, evidence refs, confidence, risks, alternatives และ timestamp หาก chain ขาดหรือมี human/Codex editorial intervention ให้สถานะ `evaluation_invalidated` โดยอัตโนมัติ

## Separation of duties

- Codex may implement and repair an algorithm, but may not choose the production's shots or timing.
- Test fixtures may contain developer-authored timelines only to test mechanics; they must be labeled `renderer_fixture`.
- The Studio Council may use OpenRouter or local AI only through configured provider adapters and data-policy gates.
- Brain/editorial engines read only validated brief snapshots, catalog metadata and Understanding Map observations; they must not open raw media.
- Clip Director must synthesize independent specialist outputs and cannot override fact, ethics, rights or technical blockers.
- Render is allowed only from a plan whose provenance says `autonomous_studio`, has candidate-bound `brief_compliance.status=pass`, and whose required gates pass or carry scoped human waivers. Legacy candidates without compliance proof fail closed.
- Structural observations such as shot boundaries are navigation evidence, not semantic evidence. A used media range must have temporally overlapping `vision`, `ocr`, `speech`, `sound` or `music` evidence.
- Deterministic validators may identify violations and gate release, but all creative repairs must be generated by the Lead Editor/Studio engines and preserved in the run ledger.
- Rights findings are advisory evidence for the responsible publisher. The system records `responsible_person_approval_required`; the person or organization that publishes the work makes the final decision and accepts responsibility. AI does not make a legal/publication decision on their behalf.

## Regression acceptance

1. A plan with `authorship=developer_fixture` cannot be promoted to studio evaluation.
2. A Studio Council run records every role response and objection before synthesis.
3. Timeline decisions reference registered asset IDs and valid source ranges.
4. Unsupported facts, missing evidence and unresolved blockers fail closed.
5. Re-running from the same immutable inputs is traceable even when model output is nondeterministic.
6. World-class remains `NOT RUN` until autonomous outputs pass blind professional review across multiple jobs.
